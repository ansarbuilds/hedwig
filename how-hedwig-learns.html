Lesson 1 · The guessing game & BPE word-pieces

An LLM only plays one game: guess the next piece

Hedwig v1 guessed one letter at a time (65 symbols). Hedwig v2/v3 uses a 4,096-piece BPE tokenizer (tokenizer.py) that glues common letters into whole words (3.87 letters/token)—so its 256-slot memory holds ~1,000 letters of story at once!

Real 4,096-vocab BPE split (69 letters → 19 word-pieces):

'Once'' upon'' a'' time'','' a'' little'' owl'' named'' H''ed''w''ig'' found'' a'' shiny'' golden'' key''.'
Lesson 2 · What a knob is

A knob is just one number

Inside Hedwig there are numbers like 0.37 or -1.2. Each one nudges a guess a little. Turn it one way and "key" gets more likely; turn it the other way and "x" does. Engineers call these numbers parameters or weights. We call them knobs.

Try it: one knob, three guesses

Drag the slider. When the knob is set right, the green bar (the right guess) is the biggest.
